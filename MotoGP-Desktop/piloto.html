Jack Miller

Jack Miller made his road race debut in 2009 after beginning his career on dirt and debuted in the 125 World Championship soon after. Taking the 125 IDM crown on his way to full time competition on the world stage, Miller first impressed in 2013 when he proved a consistent frontrunner with Racing Team Germany. A title challenge with Red Bull KTM Ajo in 2014 saw Miller just miss out to Alex Marquez, before he made the incredible move from Moto3™ straight to MotoGP™ in 2015.

After a tough rookie season, Miller took a stunning maiden win in 2016 at the TT Circuit Assen, despite a season that began with a broken leg and was then subsequently interrupted by more injury. Taking a good number of top ten results, Miller remained with the Estrella Galicia 0,0 Marc VDS team for 2017 and impressed once more before moving to Pramac for 2018 and switching from Honda to Ducati machinery.

A move to the factory squad was deserved and he delivered in 2021: two victories, three further podiums and fourth in the World Championship. Miller again proved his quality in 2022 with seven podiums, including the best MotoGP™ performance of his career when he won with ease in Japan. After five years in red, Miller donned the orange of KTM - and the season started strong with a double podium at the Spanish GP. Another Sprint podium was claimed at the German GP but it was a lowkey second half of the season.

Conceptos básicos sobre la MotoGP

Categoría Reina
MotoGP es la categoría de mayor nivel del Campeonato del Mundo de Motociclismo.
Motos Prototipo
Las motos de MotoGP son máquinas prototipo diseñadas y construidas específicamente para la competición.
FIM
Es la Federación Internacional de Motociclismo, el organismo que organiza y rige la competición.
Carrera Sprint
El sábado, antes del Gran Premio del domingo, se celebra una carrera sprint que reparte puntos adicionales y determina la parrilla de salida.
Sistema de Puntos
En una carrera principal, solo los 15 primeros pilotos obtienen puntos, mientras que en la carrera sprint solo los 9 primeros obtienen puntos.
